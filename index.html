Frequently Asked Questions

Which events are optional?

The Golf Outing, Chapter Status Convening, and Football Fellowship are optional opportunities.

Who may attend the Anniversary Banquet?

The Anniversary Banquet is a Brothers-only event.

What does the banquet cost?

The banquet is $150 per Brother and includes food plus two drink tickets for beer or wine.

Is the Football Fellowship capacity limited?

Yes. Capacity is approximately 40 Brothers.
